# Use canonical Editable Documents and preserve other files as Attachments

Viraha provides a focused rich-text and Markdown-compatible editor rather than attempt to become a universal office suite. Text, Markdown, HTML, and DOCX imports may be converted into Editable Documents, while PDFs, images, archives, and unsupported formats remain previewable or downloadable Attachments in their original form. This permits broad import and delivery without promising unsafe or lossy round-trip editing for every file format.
