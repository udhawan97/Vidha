Some messages need a careful way home.

Vidha is a contingency relay for one adult Owner to prepare recipient-specific Envelopes and rehearse what happens after a missed Check-in. A missed Check-in may enter Concern; it never proves death.

A working concept, not a service. The courier knows when to stop.

What exists today

A local, synthetic browser prototype currently stops at Concern. There is no hosted service, download, notification delivery, Recipient retrieval, Release path, tagged version, or v1. Please do not enter personal or sensitive material.

The route pauses before it moves.

The intended relay is deliberately slow. Time, provider outcomes, and human decisions stay visible instead of being compressed into one automatic leap.

Leave each person only what is theirs.

An Envelope is recipient-specific by design. Imported files are untrusted, editable conversion stays bounded, and unsupported originals remain Attachments.

Editable Document

Focused, Markdown-compatible text for the parts that should be reviewed and revised with care.

Attachment

An unsupported original stays intact. Vidha does not pretend a lossy conversion is editable.

Recipient-specific Envelope

Each Recipient is meant to authenticate before seeing only the content addressed to them.

Keep the rehearsal fictional.

The prototype uses synthetic fixtures. Do not add personal plans, contacts, documents, or delivery details.

Target design · not implemented

Automation reaches a gate. People decide what happens next.

No AI or probabilistic system may decide Check-in, Concern, Guardian quorum, Veto Window, or Release state.

Guardian Attestation
A bounded statement from a Guardian. It never asks them to declare death.
Veto Window
A full, visible period in which the Owner can stop a pending Release.
Delivery Hold
If every Owner final-notice channel fails, the Envelope waits and later starts a new full Veto Window.

The gate is intentionally shown closed. Vidha's current prototype stops at Concern; the later decision path is a documented target, not working product behavior.
