Vidha Pre-alpha · built in the open

Prepare what matters.
Release it carefully.

Vidha is an open-source contingency relay being designed for one person — the Owner — who wants selected people to receive private letters and documents if they ever become persistently unreachable. Silence starts a question for trusted humans. It is never treated as proof of death.

Where this stands: Vidha is pre-alpha. There is no hosted service, download, or release yet — this page describes the design. The repository holds a runnable synthetic prototype, the decision records behind it, and their tests.

Designed to work like this

Prepare

Write or import material in a focused workspace and seal it into recipient-specific Envelopes, each with its own Release Policy.

Check in

Quick, authenticated Check-ins on the Owner’s schedule keep everything at rest. An opened email or a scanned link never counts.

Concern

A missed schedule moves through reminders and grace, then asks Guardians — trusted people — for bounded attestations. No one is asked to declare death.

Release

Only after verification and a final veto window does an Envelope reach its Recipient. Notifications never carry the private content itself.

The lines it will not cross

  • Vidha never claims an Owner has died.
  • Nothing is released because an email was opened or a timer ran out on its own.
  • Emails and texts are notifications, not the vault.
  • No AI or probabilistic system decides Check-in, Concern, or Release.

What will make it different

Document-first

Writing and importing are the core experience, not an attachment afterthought.

A policy per Envelope

Practical instructions and deeply private letters need not share release conditions.

People, not timers

Human verification is the default; an automatic fallback is a deliberate, per-Envelope choice.

Yours to run

AGPL-3.0 and self-hostable by design, with replaceable delivery and hosting adapters.
