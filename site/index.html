Open source · recipient specific · deliberate

Rehearse a careful relay.

Vidha is a contingency relay for one adult Owner: a way to prepare recipient-specific Envelopes, make explicit Check-ins, and let a bounded human process answer prolonged silence. A missed Check-in may enter Concern. It never proves death.

What exists: a local synthetic prototype that stops at Concern. There is no hosted service, download, notification delivery, Recipient retrieval, Release path, tag, or v1 release. Never enter personal or sensitive material.

Runnable local evidence

The prototype rehearses; it does not relay.

  • Disposable by design Synthetic demo data clears with the browser session.
  • Explicit Owner Check-in Email opens, previews, and unauthenticated GET requests never count.
  • One stage at a time The controllable clock advances through On Time, Reminder, Due, and Concern.
  • No Release path Guardian Attestation, Veto Window, Delivery Hold, and Recipient retrieval are absent.

Target model · not implemented

A careful handoff needs more than a timer.

  • Recipient-specific Envelopes Each intended handoff has one Recipient, Protection Mode, and Release Policy.
  • Bounded human verification Guardian Attestations are intended to report limited facts, never declare death.
  • Owner veto remains visible Concern, final notice, and the full Veto Window cannot collapse into one time jump.
  • Private content stays out of notices A notification would lead an authenticated Recipient to content; it would not contain it.
